Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

Oye AI collects only the messages you send during a chat session. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personal identifying information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your messages are used solely to generate AI responses within your current session. We do not store, sell, or share your conversations with any third party.

3. Session Data

Chat history is stored temporarily in your browser session and is cleared when you close or refresh the page. We do not maintain any permanent logs of conversations.

4. Third Party Services

Oye AI uses Groq's API to process messages and DuckDuckGo for web search queries. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not share personally identifiable information with them.

5. Cookies

We use a minimal session cookie to maintain your chat history within a single session. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

6. Children's Privacy

Oye AI is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Oye AI after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact
